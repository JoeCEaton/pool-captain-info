Pool Captain

A pocket toolkit for league players. Your real odds before a match, how hard you actually break, how tough the table is, and the skill level you're really playing at.

I need twelve testers

Pool Captain is finished enough to use in a real match. Before it can go on the Play Store, Google requires 12 testers who stay opted in for 14 days — that's the whole reason this flyer exists.

  • An Android phone — an iPhone version isn't ready yet
  • About two minutes to sign up and install
  • Ideally you play APA 8-ball or 9-ball, or use Fargo ratings
  • Please stay installed for the full two weeks

You'll get a Play Store invite that installs like any normal download. Use it on league night, then tell me what you found — a one-line text is fine. Crashes, numbers that look wrong, anything baffling.

Seven tools, all of them offline

  • APA 8 Ball — win odds and expected match points
  • Fargo Match — odds for any race length, plus a handicap
  • Break Speed — times your break with the microphone, in mph
  • Table — rates how tough a table is, and compares rooms
  • 9 Ball Logging — the level your scoring pace really says
  • 8 Ball Logging — innings per win, graded against your level
  • The Guide — every number explained in plain language
